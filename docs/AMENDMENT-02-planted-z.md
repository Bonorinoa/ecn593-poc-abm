## Why

Wave 1 against rescaled World Bank country totals returned F-EMPTY
(\(\min I = 204 > \tau \approx 7.81\)). That finding stands for the pair
(kernel, WB-\(z\)). It is not a license to loosen \(\tau\). It *is* a reason
to stop asking this kernel to match country aggregates it cannot speak to,
and to run the PoC as a simulation study: \(z\) comes from a planted world
inside \(C\), so NROY is feasible by construction and \(\theta_{\mathrm{true}}\)
is known.

## Narrative (synthetic, real-world-shaped)

A single large village, \(N=800\) households, acquaintance network
\(G(N,4/N)\) (mean degree \(\approx 4\)). \(Y_{i,t}\in(0,1)\) is the intensity
of a **preventive health practice** (clinic-visit / hygiene / vaccine-compliance
score — continuous, not a country coverage rate).

- \(x_1\): health literacy / education proxy
- \(x_2\): household resources
- \(x_3\): baseline health propensity (treatment-channel covariate; \(m_3\) loads here)

A campaign can home-visit 10% of households from \(t^\star=0\):

- \(\pi_0\): no campaign
- \(\pi_1\): 80 households at random (SRSWOR)
- \(\pi_2\): the 80 highest-degree households (village infuencers / CHW candidates)

Moments (same three slots as before, now generated by the DGP):

| slot | functional | village reading |
|---|---|---|
| \(z_1\) / `level_imm` | mean \(\bar Y_T\) under \(\pi_2\) | practice level after hub campaign |
| \(z_2\) / `gradient_edu` | \(\mathrm{corr}(Y_T,x_1)\) under \(\pi_2\) | literacy gradient of practice |
| \(z_3\) / `level_internet` | mean \(\bar Y_T\) under \(\pi_1\) | practice level after random campaign |

Nothing here is a World Bank series. The names in code are historical.

## Planted world

\(c^\star\) is the **center of the frozen box** \(C\) (midpoint of each
interval). No coordinate is chosen by looking at \(\theta\).

\[
c^\star = (-1.5,\; 0.325,\; 0.425,\; 1.375,\; 0.205)
\]

in \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\kappa,\sigma_\varepsilon)\). \(\eta\) stays
\((0.05,0.10,0.02)\).

\(z := \widehat M(c^\star)\) with \(R_z = R_{\mathrm{estimand}} = 200\) paired
reps. The realized \((z,\sigma^2_{\mathrm{sampling}})\) is frozen in
`data/planted.json` before waves. Sampling variances are the Monte Carlo
variances of that 200-rep mean (diagonal). \(\delta\) and \(\tau\) are
**not** touched: \(V=\mathrm{diag}(\sigma^2_{\mathrm{samp}}(z)+\sigma^2_{\mathrm{MC}}(\widehat M)+\delta^2)\).

Keeping \(\delta\) on a correctly specified DGP is conservative ruling-out
(NROY larger than a \(\delta=0\) match). That is a pre-declared analyst
budget, not a rescue.
